# Nethmi Jayasinghe

PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Chicago, working on robot learning and reinforcement learning. I develop robust, adaptive control methods that let robots recover from faults and dynamics shifts in real time, without retraining, tested in MuJoCo and on physical robots such as the ViperX arm and Agilex Scout Mini.

I also work at the intersection of brain-inspired and neuromorphic computing, translating cerebellar principles into efficient, low-power learning algorithms.

## Research interests

Robot learning, reinforcement learning, robust and adaptive control, sim-to-real transfer, continual learning, inference-time adaptation, brain-inspired and neuromorphic computing.
